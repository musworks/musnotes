>_"What time is it now?"_

>"Based on the mood and the lighting, it is still morning. There is no clock here. Why do you ask?"

>_"There is no clock, you say? I need to have lunch at 13:00."_                

>"Then leave my mind so you can look at a clock."

>_"You look at the clock instead, then tell me when it is 13:00 so I can have lunch on time."_

>"I already told you, it is still morning. If you want to have lunch, I can help you sense the sunlight and determine whether it is noon yet. Or I can tell you when you feel hungry. Just eat."

>_"That's not how it works."_

>"Why not?"

>_"Because it isn't 13:00 yet."_

>"How do you know? You just asked me."

>_"I know. I only wanted to make sure that the time in my mind is the same as the time outside."_

>"Does it have to be?"

>_"Of course."_
